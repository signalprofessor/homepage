# What makes a problem worth doing?

I see more potentially interesting research problems than I could pursue in several lifetimes. So I need a filter. It has four S's—in Swedish, **fyra ess i leken**.

**Samhällsnytta — the world.** Does it matter? Something useful should happen if we succeed. There should be a reason beyond producing another paper.

**Socialt — the people.** Do I want to do it with these people? Research consumes too much of life to spend it with people you don't enjoy.

**Svårt som fan — the problem.** Is it genuinely difficult? If somebody else can easily solve it, they should. I can play golf instead.

**Spännande — me.** Do I actually want to know the answer? There are infinitely many difficult and useful problems. This one has to make me curious enough to act.

**The world. The people. The problem. Me.**
