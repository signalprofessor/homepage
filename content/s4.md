# So why all these things?

Change detection. Cars. Particle filters. Mobile phones. Companies. Rhinos. Pandemics. Chess. Drones. Heartbeats.

There are more interesting problems than I could possibly work on.

Over the years I seem to have developed a filter. The S4 rule.

## Samhällsnytta · Societal benefit

### The world.

**Does it matter?**

Something useful should happen if we succeed. There should be a reason beyond producing another paper.

## Socialt · People

### The people.

**Do I want to do it with these people?**

Research consumes too much of life to spend it with people you don't enjoy.

## Svårt som fan · Difficult as hell

### The problem.

**Is it genuinely difficult?**

If somebody else can easily solve it, they should.

I can play golf instead.

## Spännande · Exciting

### Me.

**Do I actually want to know the answer?**

There are infinitely many difficult and useful problems. This one has to make *me* curious enough to act.

> **The world. The people. The problem. Me.**
