# Occasionally found in trees.

Project Ngulia brought smartphones, sensors, cameras, radar and drones into ranger operations at a Kenyan rhino sanctuary. The objective was never a better gadget. It was a better decision available to the people protecting wildlife.

**Field equipment** — Trail cameras · Tracking tags · Laptop · Drone · Espresso machine

During the pandemic I once supervised a licentiate defence remotely from a leopard cliff in Tsavo. Connectivity was adequate. The battery-powered espresso machine also worked.
