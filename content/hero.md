# Find the signal in the noise

Fredrik Gustafsson is a signal professor in the broadest sense: someone who looks for meaningful structure in noisy reality. He likes problems that matter, people he enjoys, challenges that are difficult as hell, and ideas exciting enough to steal time from golf.

He moves between mathematics and field reality with little respect for disciplinary borders, turning uncertain measurements into decisions, research into systems, and occasionally equations into companies.

Nature, competition, family, humour and a portable espresso machine belong to the same story.

Having achieved most of the conventional things he once aimed for, he is now in the fortunate and slightly dangerous position of being free to see what turns up next.
