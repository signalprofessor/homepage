# Out of office

I grew up in Aneby.

Before there were wildlife sensors, there was a boy looking for birds and playing football.

Chess took over when I was thirteen. Running came at university. Golf arrived eventually.

![Fredrik Gustafsson approaching the finish of his first marathon in 1987, wearing period running clothes.](imagemarathon.png "First marathon, 1987. 100 metres to go. || This was before I realised that dedicated running gear existed.")

Four children arrived over a rather longer interval.

And espresso appears to have become infrastructure.

![Fredrik Gustafsson with two people inside the first Starbucks in Spain before its opening in Barcelona in 2002.](imagestarbucks.png "Barcelona, 2002. Looking for an espresso.")

The first Starbucks in Spain was opening the next morning. I found it the night before, tried the locked door and looked sufficiently disappointed that the CEO let me in.

He and the chairman had flown in from Seattle for the opening.

**So we had an espresso.**
