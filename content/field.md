# Occasionally, the equations escape the university

Cars, aircraft, smartphones, audio, positioning, drones, ships, wildlife and conservation may look like unrelated applications. The common thread is inference under uncertainty—and the urge to test whether the inference survives contact with reality.

**Observe → model → estimate → build → deploy → hand over.**

The endpoint matters. A system, company or research project succeeds when it becomes useful to someone else and no longer needs permanent control by its originator.
