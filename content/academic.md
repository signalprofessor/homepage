# For the record.

Professor at Linköping University since 1999, currently Professor of Sensor Informatics. Research in statistical signal processing, sensor fusion, positioning and tracking, with an unfortunate tendency to spread into cars, aircraft, phones, audio, drones and wildlife.

Author of books including *Adaptive Filtering and Change Detection* and *Statistical Sensor Fusion*. IEEE Fellow, recipient of the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences' Arnberg Prize, and member of the Royal Swedish Academy of Engineering Sciences.
