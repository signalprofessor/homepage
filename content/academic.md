# Less random statistics

Professor of Sensor Informatics at Linköping University since 2005; previously appointed professor of Communication Systems in 1999.

Research spans statistical signal processing, adaptive filtering and change detection, with applications in communications, vehicles, aircraft and audio.

Author of books including *Adaptive Filtering and Change Detection*, *Signal Processing* and *Statistical Sensor Fusion*.

Awarded the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences' Arnberg Prize in 2004 and elected to the Royal Swedish Academy of Engineering Sciences in 2007.

Initiated Wildlife Security with Johan Bergenäs; LiU's Project Ngulia work connected mobile reporting, sensors and drones with ranger operations.
